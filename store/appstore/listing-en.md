## Description (4000 / 1013)

```
A puzzle played on grid paper, with pencil-drawn number balls.

■ How to play
· Tap a ball in your hand to load it onto the rail.
· Drag to aim, release to shoot.
· A red border means prime. Hit it and it vanishes.
· A composite splits if your ball divides it, and grows by that number if it does not.
· Clear every ball on the table to finish the stage.

■ Learn to read numbers
91 looks prime, but it is 7 x 13. Shoot it wrong and it only gets bigger. The whole game is telling what can still be divided from what cannot.

■ What you get
· 12 stages. More balls and bigger numbers as you go.
· One shot that hits several balls in a row builds a combo and scores more.
· Stuck? Spend a hint to see a prime factorization. You earn one every time you clear a stage.
· Post your score to the Game Center leaderboard (optional).
· Korean and English, following your device language.

■ No strings
· No ads.
· No in-app purchases.
· No account needed, and it plays offline (a connection is only used to post a score).
```
